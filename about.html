Our Story

This didn't begin as a business idea.
It became personal first.

After more than 25 years producing national television commercials for some of the world's best-known companies, I never imagined that the most important thing I would ever produce would be joy for my own mother.

Before Elite Legacy

My mother was not someone who needed to be kept busy.

After my father passed away, I moved back into my family home in La Jolla, California. My mother was vibrant, independent and deeply engaged with life. She was a Master Bridge player and spent much of her week doing something she genuinely loved with people she enjoyed.

Then her health changed quickly.

Medicine understandably focused on the physical crisis. But as I watched what was happening, something else became impossible for me to ignore:

Her body needed care. Her spirit still needed a life.

I began doing what I knew how to do. I produced.

Bridge with her closest friends. Lunches. Dinners. People coming through the door. Things to talk about. Things to anticipate. In one of her final weeks, six of her friends gathered in her bedroom for one of her favorite meals — lobster dinner.

I wasn't treating her illness. I was trying to make sure illness didn't get to become the only thing happening in her life.

The Realization

That experience never left me.

It showed me how easily an older person's world can become organized around what is wrong, what needs attention and what can no longer be done.

But the person is still there.

The stories are still there. The humor is still there. The curiosity is still there. The desire to be known — rather than managed — is still there.

Years later, that became the beginning of Elite Legacy.

A Slightly Selfish Reason

And then I asked myself a question.

When I get to this stage in life, what would I want?

I would want someone interesting to talk to. Someone who wanted to hear the rest of the story. Someone who would laugh with me, cook with me, play a game, take a walk, go somewhere, or simply sit down and have a real conversation.

I would want someone who was interested in who I still am — not simply what I can no longer do.

And I would want something to look forward to tomorrow.

Why My Background Matters

Producing the experience is something I've spent a career doing.

My professional life included more than 300 national television campaigns for major brands, creative development with some of the world's leading advertising agencies, budgeting, scheduling, production, pricing and response testing, sales systems, national accounts and taking ideas from concept to measurable marketplace execution.

Elite Legacy applies a very different purpose to many of those same skills: understand the person, notice what matters, create the experience carefully, and never confuse activity with impact.

Personal by Design

Small by design.
Personal on purpose.

Elite Legacy is intentionally personal because meaningful companionship depends on knowing the person.

What makes them laugh. What fascinates them. The stories they love telling. The things they haven't done in years. The places they would still like to go. The meal they haven't cooked since their children were young.

We want to know their family, too — and understand what they hope these years can become.

Elite Legacy is built around personal involvement, carefully selected Companions and an uncompromising standard for the experience we create.

When a family entrusts us with someone they love, personal isn't an upgrade. It's the standard.
A note about discretion. The families we serve are entitled to privacy. We do not publicly identify clients or share their personal stories without explicit permission, and we do not provide client names or contact information as references. Their privacy is part of our standard.
